Programs

Programs that turn talent into lasting opportunity.

Lonestar Sports Foundation supports young athletes through scholarship access, competition pathways, community outreach, and international student support.

Lonestar Programs

Student Scholarships

Empowering young athletes to excel both in the classroom and on the court. We provide financial assistance to dedicated students, ensuring that financial constraints never stand in the way of their academic dreams and athletic potential.

Tournaments and Event Sponsorship

Creating competitive pathways and removing barriers to entry. By backing local tournaments and sponsoring entry fees, we give talented players the stage they need to compete, grow, and showcase their skills.

Community Outreach

Bringing the joy and health benefits of racket sports directly to our neighborhoods. Through free local clinics, school partnerships, and open-play events, we build vibrant connections and make sports accessible to everyone.

International Student Support Programs

Helping global student-athletes find their footing and thrive in a new home. We offer dedicated mentorship, community integration, and logistical support to ensure international students transition smoothly into both their academic and athletic journeys.

Program partnerships are welcome

We are continuing to build these programs with community partners, sponsors, schools, volunteers, and families who care about youth access through sport.
