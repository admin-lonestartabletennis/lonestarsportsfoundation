Featured impact story

Youth Table Tennis Summer Camp

A place for young athletes to learn, play, build confidence, and belong.

See the impact +

Camp experiences connect players with coaching, peers, and a positive environment where effort and growth matter. Support helps make those experiences more reachable for families and emerging athletes.

  • Skill-building through structured table tennis activities
  • Encouragement, teamwork, and confidence on and off the table
  • A welcoming community for young players and families

Competitive pathway

Tournament & Event Sponsorship

Helping talented players get to the table when cost could otherwise keep them out.

See the impact +

Tournament participation gives young athletes a meaningful way to test their skills, learn from competition, and set new goals. Sponsorship can remove practical barriers such as registration and event-related costs.

  • More access to competitive experiences
  • New chances to practice resilience and sportsmanship
  • A visible pathway for developing athletes

Community connection

Community Play & Outreach

Creating welcoming introductions to sport for young people, families, and neighbors.

See the impact +

Community events can turn a first introduction to sport into a lasting connection. They create low-pressure spaces for young people to try something new and discover the people around them who will cheer them on.

  • Accessible opportunities to try table tennis
  • Strong connections among players, families, and mentors
  • A foundation for future participation and leadership
