About Us

Expanding access to high-character youth development through sport.

Lonestar Sports Foundation is building a stronger path for young athletes by pairing sports training with discipline, resilience, mentorship, and community support.

Who we are

Lonestar Sports Foundation exists to open doors for talented youth who need access, structure, coaching, and encouragement. We believe sports can shape habits and character that influence every part of a young person’s future.

Our work is grounded in long-term development, not just competition. We want young people to grow as athletes, teammates, students, and leaders.
