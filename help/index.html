How You Can Help

Help young athletes grow through access, guidance, and opportunity.

Lonestar Sports Foundation welcomes supporters who want to expand youth access to sport, mentorship, and character-building programs in the Austin community.

Support the next stage of youth development

Your support can help reduce barriers for talented youth, strengthen program access, and create a welcoming environment where young athletes can build confidence, discipline, and leadership.

Volunteer

Share time and energy at community events, youth activities, facility days, and foundation programs.

Partner

Collaborate with us through schools, clubs, local businesses, and community groups that care about youth opportunity.

Sponsor

Help offset program costs, equipment needs, training access, and future scholarship opportunities.

Start a conversation

Email us to discuss volunteer interest, sponsorship ideas, donor questions, or ways your organization can support youth development through sport.
